Affordability is more than getting approved

A lender may use different income definitions, credit checks and country-specific underwriting rules. Carrowmont does not predict approval and does not use one universal price-to-income or payment-to-income cutoff.

HOW IT WORKS

Three Carrowmont engines, one purchase decision

Loan / EMI calculates the financed payment and total interest. Borrowing Health shows the effect on debt-to-income and monthly cash room. Emergency Fund & Runway shows how the purchase changes the cash buffer against ongoing needs.

Running costs are added separately because insurance, fuel or charging, maintenance and parking can materially change the monthly picture even when the loan payment looks manageable.

What Carrowmont does not assume

  • It does not use a universal 20/4/10, payment-to-income or vehicle-price-to-income rule.
  • It does not predict lender approval, credit score, insurance premiums or resale value.
  • It does not estimate taxes or registration unless you enter the upfront amount.
  • It does not model depreciation, investment opportunity cost or a trade-in loan balance.
  • It does not send or store the financial amounts entered on this page.

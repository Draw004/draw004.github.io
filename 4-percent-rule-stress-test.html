What this tool does

It models a fixed first-year portfolio withdrawal that increases with inflation, then compares smooth, cautious and weak-early-return paths.

It does not: forecast markets, calculate a probability of success or identify a universally safe rate.

METHOD

Transparent annual mechanics

  1. Record the opening portfolio.
  2. Take the inflation-adjusted withdrawal at the start of the year.
  3. Apply that year's deterministic return to the remaining balance.
  4. Increase the next withdrawal using that scenario's inflation assumption.

FAQ

Important questions about the stress test

Does this tool prove that 4% is safe?

No. It is a deterministic educational stress test. It does not estimate a probability of success or guarantee that any withdrawal rate will work.

Why can the order of returns change the result?

Withdrawals remove money while the portfolio is changing. Losses early in retirement can leave less capital available for later recovery, even when the same annual returns occur in a different order.

Why is the adverse return sequence synthetic?

The purpose is to isolate a disclosed stress pattern without pretending it is historical data. The first ten annual returns are normalized so their geometric average matches your selected long-run return.

Does Carrowmont store the values I enter?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser. No account or cloud storage is required.

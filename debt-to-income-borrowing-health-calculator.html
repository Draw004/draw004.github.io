HOW IT WORKS

Two views instead of one misleading score

Gross-income DTI = required monthly debt payments divided by gross monthly income. If you add another payment, Carrowmont shows the ratio again with that payment included.

Cash room uses take-home income, debt payments and the essential non-debt costs you enter. This helps reveal pressure that a gross-income ratio alone can hide.

What Carrowmont does not assume

  • It does not use one DTI percentage as a universal safe or unsafe cutoff.
  • It does not predict lender approval, credit limits or borrowing rates.
  • It does not model taxes, benefits, country-specific underwriting rules or credit scoring.
  • It does not treat unused credit as income or financial room.
  • It does not send or store the financial amounts entered on this page.

CARROWMONT RESEARCH

Data-led research for clearer financial decisions.

Carrowmont Research combines authoritative public datasets with transparent, reproducible calculations. The goal is not to predict markets; it is to show how financial conditions change the decisions households actually face.

How Carrowmont Research works

1. Start with primary dataOfficial or authoritative sources are identified with dates and geographic scope.
2. Separate data from modelCarrowmont calculations are labelled as calculations, not reported facts or forecasts.
3. Publish the evidenceKey assumptions, formulas, charts and downloadable calculation data remain visible.

Corrections and media enquiries

If you spot a source, calculation or presentation issue, please tell us. Research corrections are handled through the normal Carrowmont contact process.

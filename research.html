How Carrowmont Research works

1. Start with primary dataOfficial or authoritative sources are identified with dates and geographic scope.
2. Separate data from modelCarrowmont calculations are labelled as calculations, not reported facts or forecasts.
3. Publish the evidenceKey assumptions, formulas, charts and downloadable calculation data remain visible.

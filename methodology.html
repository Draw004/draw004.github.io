Our Methodology

Transparent assumptions.
Understandable calculations.

Financial projections are only as useful as the assumptions behind them. Carrowmont aims to make those assumptions clear and allow users to explore how different scenarios may affect the outcome.

Our approach

Carrowmont tools are designed primarily for financial education, planning and scenario analysis.

Rather than treating a financial projection as a prediction, we aim to show what may happen under a particular set of assumptions.

Visible assumptions

Important inputs such as inflation, growth rates and time horizons should be visible whenever practical.

Scenario based

Users should be able to explore how changing assumptions can change projected outcomes.

Educational

Results should help explain financial relationships, not simply provide a number.

Financial assumptions

Many financial calculations require assumptions about future conditions. These may include:

  • inflation
  • investment returns
  • interest rates
  • income growth
  • expense growth
  • retirement age
  • life expectancy
  • tax treatment
  • future contributions or withdrawals

These factors cannot be known with certainty in advance. Where possible, Carrowmont tools allow users to modify important assumptions rather than relying entirely on fixed default values.

Default values

Some tools may provide default values to make the calculator easier to use.

A default value should be treated as a starting assumption, not as a forecast or recommendation.

As Carrowmont develops, methodology notes for individual tools may explain how important default assumptions were selected and, where appropriate, identify the data or reference sources used.

Inflation

Inflation reduces the purchasing power of money over time. For this reason, many Carrowmont planning tools may distinguish between today's value of money and future nominal amounts.

Illustrative example

If an expense costs ₹50,000 per month today, the same lifestyle may require a much higher nominal amount many years from now if prices continue to rise.

The exact future cost depends on the inflation assumption and the period being considered.

Investment returns

Investment returns are uncertain and can vary significantly from year to year.

When a Carrowmont tool uses an assumed return, that return is intended for scenario modelling rather than as a promise, forecast or recommendation.

Actual investment performance may be substantially higher or lower than an assumed rate.

Retirement projections

Retirement planning involves many variables, including current spending, future inflation, retirement income, investment performance, major expenses and the length of retirement.

Carrowmont's retirement tools are intended to help users explore how those variables interact.

A retirement projection should therefore be interpreted as an illustrative scenario rather than a guarantee that a particular amount will be sufficient.

Rounding

Some values displayed by Carrowmont may be rounded to improve readability.

Calculations may use more precise values internally than the figures displayed on screen. As a result, totals may occasionally differ slightly from calculations performed manually using only the rounded figures shown.

Taxes and regulations

Tax laws, financial regulations and government schemes can change over time and may vary depending on a user's individual circumstances.

Any tax-related calculations or explanations published by Carrowmont should be considered educational unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Users should confirm important tax, legal or regulatory matters with an appropriately qualified professional or official source.

Data and accuracy

We aim to make Carrowmont calculations understandable and internally consistent. However, errors can occur and financial rules, tax provisions and economic conditions can change.

Important financial decisions should therefore not rely solely on a Carrowmont calculator or article.

Improving our methodology

Carrowmont is an evolving platform. Calculators, assumptions, formulas and explanations may be updated as the platform develops or as better information becomes available.

Where significant methodology changes affect a tool, we intend to make the relevant assumptions and calculation approach as understandable as reasonably possible.

Important: Carrowmont provides educational information, planning tools and illustrative calculations. Nothing on the platform should be interpreted as a guarantee of financial outcomes or as individualized investment, financial, legal or tax advice.

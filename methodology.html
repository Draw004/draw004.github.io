Our methodology

Transparent assumptions. Understandable calculations.

Financial projections are only as useful as the assumptions behind them. Carrowmont aims to make those assumptions clear and allow users to explore how different scenarios may affect the outcome.

Visible assumptions

Where practical, Carrowmont exposes key assumptions such as time horizon, inflation, investment returns, retirement spending and income rather than hiding them inside the model.

Scenario analysis

Planning tools are designed to help users compare possibilities. Results are estimates based on the information entered and should not be interpreted as predictions or guarantees.

Today’s money and future money

Carrowmont distinguishes between amounts expressed in today’s purchasing power and nominal future currency values. Tool labels are designed to show which type of value is being displayed.

Country and currency

The country selector controls context and default currency formatting. Switching currencies does not automatically perform foreign-exchange conversion. Country-specific taxes, pensions, accounts and legal rules are not assumed unless explicitly described by a tool.

Limitations

Actual inflation, investment returns, taxes, longevity, healthcare costs and market conditions can differ materially from assumptions. Users should treat outputs as educational estimates and consider qualified professional advice where appropriate.

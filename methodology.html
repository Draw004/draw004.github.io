Our Methodology

Transparent assumptions.
Understandable calculations.

Financial projections are only as useful as the assumptions behind them. Carrowmont aims to make those assumptions visible, understandable and easy to explore.

Our approach

Carrowmont tools are designed primarily for financial education, planning and scenario analysis.

A financial projection is not a prediction of exactly what will happen. It shows what may happen when a particular set of assumptions is applied.

Our aim is therefore to help users understand the relationship between their inputs, the assumptions used and the resulting financial projection.

Visible assumptions

Important inputs such as inflation, returns and time horizons should be visible whenever practical.

Scenario based

Users should be able to explore how changing assumptions affects projected financial outcomes.

Educational

Results should help explain financial relationships rather than simply produce a final number.

Financial assumptions

Many financial calculations require assumptions about future conditions.

Depending on the tool, these may include:

  • inflation
  • investment returns
  • interest rates
  • income growth
  • expense growth
  • retirement age
  • life expectancy
  • future contributions
  • future withdrawals
  • tax treatment

These variables cannot be known with certainty in advance.

Where practical, Carrowmont aims to allow users to modify important assumptions rather than rely completely on fixed values.

Default values

Some Carrowmont tools may include default assumptions to make them easier to use.

A default value should be interpreted as a starting point for calculation rather than a forecast or recommendation.

Where appropriate, individual tools may explain how important default assumptions were selected and identify relevant reference sources.

Inflation

Inflation reduces the purchasing power of money over time.

Because of this, many Carrowmont planning tools may distinguish between money expressed in today's purchasing power and future nominal amounts.

Illustrative example

An expense of ₹50,000 per month today could require a substantially larger nominal amount many years from now if prices continue to rise.

The projected future cost depends on both the assumed inflation rate and the period being considered.

Investment returns

Investment returns are uncertain and can vary significantly from year to year.

When a Carrowmont tool uses an assumed investment return, the assumption is intended for scenario modelling.

It should not be interpreted as a guaranteed return, market forecast or investment recommendation.

Actual investment performance may be substantially higher or lower than the assumptions used in a calculation.

Retirement projections

Retirement planning involves many interconnected variables.

These can include current expenses, future inflation, retirement income, investment returns, one-time expenses, retirement age and the duration of retirement.

Carrowmont's retirement planning tools are intended to help users explore how these variables interact.

A retirement projection should therefore be treated as an illustrative financial scenario rather than a guarantee that a particular amount will be sufficient.

Rounding

Some values displayed on Carrowmont may be rounded to improve readability.

Calculations may use more precise values internally than the figures displayed on screen.

As a result, manually calculating totals using rounded displayed numbers may occasionally produce a slightly different result.

Taxes, regulations and government schemes

Tax laws, financial regulations and government schemes can change over time.

They may also vary according to an individual's circumstances, location, residency status or other factors.

Tax-related tools or explanations published by Carrowmont should therefore be treated as educational unless clearly stated otherwise.

Important tax, legal or regulatory matters should be confirmed using appropriate official sources or qualified professional advice.

Data sources

Where Carrowmont relies on external information for important assumptions, reference values or financial rules, our aim is to use credible sources wherever reasonably possible.

These may include government agencies, regulators, central banks, official financial institutions or other reputable sources.

Data may change after publication, so users should independently verify information that may materially affect an important financial decision.

Accuracy and limitations

Carrowmont aims to make its calculations understandable and internally consistent.

However, errors can occur and financial rules, economic conditions and data may change.

Important financial decisions should therefore not rely solely on a Carrowmont calculator, projection or article.

Improving our methodology

Carrowmont is an evolving platform.

Calculators, assumptions, formulas and explanations may be improved as the platform develops or as better information becomes available.

Where significant methodology changes affect a tool, our aim is to make the relevant assumptions and calculation approach understandable to users.

Important: Carrowmont provides educational information, planning tools and illustrative calculations. Nothing on the platform should be interpreted as a guarantee of financial outcomes or as individualized investment, financial, legal, accounting or tax advice.

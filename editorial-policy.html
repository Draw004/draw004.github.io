Editorial & review policy

Clear standards for how Carrowmont publishes financial information.

This policy explains how Carrowmont approaches educational content, calculator changes, external sources, quality checks, corrections and review labels.

Educational purpose

Carrowmont publishes financial education, calculators and scenario tools to help people understand assumptions and possible outcomes. Content is general and educational; it is not individualized investment, financial, legal, accounting or tax advice.

Calculator standards

Calculator logic should make important assumptions visible, use documented formulas or decision rules, and distinguish illustrative scenarios from predictions. Material changes are checked against expected calculation behavior and relevant user-facing outputs before release.

Content standards

Educational pages should explain the concept being discussed, avoid unsupported certainty, separate facts from modeled assumptions, and connect readers to relevant methodology or tools when useful. We aim to use plain language without hiding material limitations.

Sources and reference data

Where external facts, market data or official reference values materially support a page, Carrowmont aims to use authoritative or primary sources where practical and to show source or reference dates when they matter. Data can be revised or published on different schedules, so a displayed value may not represent a live market quote.

Quality checks before release

Carrowmont uses a controlled release process and automated checks where practical. Depending on the change, checks can cover deterministic calculation examples, metadata and structured-data contracts, links, page behavior, responsive layout, reports and cross-repository consistency. Automated checks reduce implementation risk but cannot guarantee that every page is error-free.

Professional review labels

Carrowmont does not present content as reviewed by a named financial professional unless that review has actually occurred and the reviewer can be identified appropriately. The absence of a professional-review label should not be read as professional certification or personalized advice.

Corrections and updates

If a calculation, factual statement, source reference or page behavior appears wrong, users can report it through Contact Carrowmont. Material issues are reviewed and, when confirmed, may be corrected with the page or source date updated as appropriate.

Related policies

See the Carrowmont Methodology for how assumptions and scenario outputs are presented, and the Disclaimer for important limits on the use of Carrowmont information.

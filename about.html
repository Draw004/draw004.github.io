About Carrowmont

Better financial decisions start with understanding the numbers.

Carrowmont is being built to make financial planning easier to understand through practical tools, transparent calculations and clear financial education.

Why Carrowmont exists

Financial decisions often involve complicated calculations, assumptions and terminology. Carrowmont aims to make those inputs visible so people can understand how a result was produced and explore different scenarios before making their own decisions.

What we are building

Carrowmont combines financial calculators, planning tools and educational resources. Current tools include the Retirement Planner and Inflation Calculator, with additional goal-planning and financial-independence tools planned over time.

Built for different countries

Carrowmont supports country-aware currency formatting so people can work in a familiar financial context. Country and currency selection changes presentation, not exchange rates, tax rules or country-specific forecasts unless a tool explicitly says otherwise.

Educational first. Carrowmont provides educational and illustrative information. It does not provide individualized investment, financial, legal, accounting or tax advice.

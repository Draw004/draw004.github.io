Reference point, not a rule

Official consumer-finance guidance commonly uses roughly 3 to 6 months of regular expenses as a starting reference. Your useful target can be lower or higher depending on income stability, dependants, fixed commitments, insurance and how quickly income could recover.

HOW IT WORKS

Transparent arithmetic, not a hidden safety score

Current runway = emergency-ready reserve divided by essential monthly need. Your target amount is your essential monthly need multiplied by the number of months you choose.

The stress test subtracts the one-time shock from the reserve and then measures how long the remainder may cover the monthly shortfall after any continuing income you enter.

What Carrowmont does not assume

  • It does not assume investments can always be sold safely at the value you expect.
  • It does not count unused credit as emergency savings.
  • It does not choose an exact emergency-fund target for you.
  • It does not model tax, benefits eligibility, insurance claims or country-specific support systems.
  • It does not send or store the financial amounts entered on this page.

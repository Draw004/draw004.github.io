## One-sentence finding

U.S. headline CPI was 3.7% in September 2026, but the eight BLS major household expenditure groups ranged from 1.5% for medical care to 7.9% for transportation, while the CPI-U price level implies that matching $100 of September 2016 purchasing power would require about $139.56 in September 2026.

## Useful factual angles

- Why one headline CPI number can feel different from a household's lived spending mix.
- Transportation at 7.9% versus medical care at 1.5% across the eight BLS major expenditure groups.
- What $100 of September 2016 purchasing power costs in September 2026 dollars.
- Why a transportation-heavy household can produce a higher weighted inflation illustration than the BLS major-group mix without claiming an official personal CPI.
- Selected price pressures: gasoline, energy and airline fares versus categories that moved more slowly or declined.

# Carrowmont SEO3 — Authority & Organic Traffic Growth Plan

**Plan date:** 9 October 2026  
**Planning horizon:** 90 days  
**Status:** Proposed execution plan after BING-SEO1 and BING-SEO2  

## 1. Purpose

Carrowmont has completed the technical indexing foundation: IndexNow, accurate sitemap freshness, stronger internal linking, improved titles and descriptions, and QA protection. SEO3 moves from technical repair to **authority, referral traffic, and demand-led content growth**.

The objective is not to publish more pages for the sake of volume. The objective is to earn credible mentions and links, create assets that people genuinely want to reference, and deepen the topics where Bing has already begun testing Carrowmont.

## 2. Baseline

Observed early Bing themes supplied by the owner include:

- financial independence number;
- what is my financial independence number;
- 4 percent retirement rule and risk of running out of savings;
- pay yourself first budgeting definition;
- inflation calculator / currency-related searches.

The impression counts remain very small, so the current average positions should be treated as directional signals rather than stable rankings.

## 3. Non-negotiable rules

- No bulk backlink packages, private blog networks, link farms, automated guest-post spam, or paid links intended to manipulate rankings.
- No fake authors, invented credentials, fabricated statistics, or mass-produced pages created only for search traffic.
- No new page unless it serves a distinct user need and fits an approved topic cluster.
- Every outreach request must be relevant, personalized, and based on a useful Carrowmont resource.
- Core tools stay free and accessible without login.
- Search growth must not change approved calculator mathematics.

## 4. 90-day objectives

These are working targets, not guarantees:

1. Publish three link-worthy Carrowmont assets.
2. Earn 5–10 new relevant referring domains, with quality prioritized over quantity.
3. Secure at least 3 editorial/resource mentions or links from genuinely relevant sites.
4. Establish four focused topical clusters: Financial Independence, Retirement/4% Rule, Budgeting/Pay Yourself First, and Inflation/Currency.
5. Create a repeatable outreach process with a target list, contact history, follow-ups, and outcomes.
6. Use Bing data to improve pages only after enough impressions exist to support a decision.

## 5. Strategy pillars

### Pillar A — Link-worthy assets

#### Asset 1: 4% Rule Stress Test

**Priority:** First  
**Purpose:** Turn the existing 4% Rule content into a transparent interactive reference showing why a fixed withdrawal rule can fail under inflation, poor early returns, or longer retirement periods.

Recommended features:

- base, cautious, and adverse scenarios;
- printed annual values rather than hover-only charts;
- methodology and assumptions;
- worked examples in multiple currencies;
- downloadable summary;
- direct links to Retirement Planner and Inflation Calculator.

#### Asset 2: Financial Independence Number by Spending

**Purpose:** Provide a clear table and interactive model showing how annual spending assumptions affect the FI target.

Recommended features:

- country/currency support;
- annual and monthly spending views;
- multiple withdrawal-rate illustrations;
- inflation-adjusted explanation;
- link to the full FI Planner.

#### Asset 3: Pay Yourself First Budget Template

**Purpose:** Create a practical, shareable budgeting resource linked to the Budget & Cash Flow Planner.

Recommended features:

- printable/downloadable template;
- worked example;
- monthly and pay-cycle versions;
- protected categories and irregular-bill reserves;
- clear connection to Smart Suggestions.

### Pillar B — Topical authority clusters

#### Cluster 1: Financial Independence

Core page: FI Planner / FI Number guide.

Supporting topics:

- how to calculate an FI number;
- FI number by spending level;
- FI number versus retirement target;
- how inflation changes the FI number;
- how today’s expenses affect FI timing.

#### Cluster 2: Retirement and the 4% Rule

Core page: Retirement Planner / 4% Rule Stress Test.

Supporting topics:

- why the 4% rule can fail;
- sequence-of-returns risk;
- retirement runway;
- inflation and retirement spending;
- safe-withdrawal assumptions and limitations.

#### Cluster 3: Budgeting and Pay Yourself First

Core page: Budget & Cash Flow Planner / Pay Yourself First template.

Supporting topics:

- pay yourself first definition;
- monthly versus pay-cycle budgeting;
- annual and quarterly bill reserves;
- protected expenses;
- emergency reserve planning.

#### Cluster 4: Inflation and Currency

Core page: Inflation Calculator.

Supporting topics:

- future purchasing power;
- inflation calculator by currency;
- nominal versus real value;
- how inflation changes goals, retirement, and FI assumptions.

### Pillar C — Authority outreach

Priority audiences:

1. Personal-finance bloggers and FIRE/retirement writers.
2. Financial-literacy educators, nonprofit resource pages, and university financial-wellness programs.
3. Employee financial-wellness and HR-benefits publishers.
4. Fintech/tool directories and product-discovery platforms.
5. Journalists and newsletter writers covering personal finance, retirement, and budgeting.
6. Relevant communities where the resource genuinely answers a question.

Outreach cadence:

- 10–15 carefully selected contacts per week;
- one personalized first message;
- one follow-up after 5–7 days;
- no further follow-up unless the recipient engages;
- track the target page, contact, date, response, outcome, and referring URL.

### Pillar D — Trust and authorship

Before major outreach, confirm that Carrowmont clearly communicates:

- who is responsible for the site;
- how calculator methodology is developed and reviewed;
- that outputs are educational illustrations, not individualized financial advice;
- when a page was last reviewed;
- which primary sources support factual claims;
- how automation or AI assistance is used where reasonably expected.

### Pillar E — Measurement and iteration

Weekly checks:

- Bing indexed URL count and exclusion reasons;
- Bing search impressions, clicks, CTR, queries, and pages;
- Bing backlink report: referring domains, source URLs, target URLs, and anchor text;
- IndexNow workflow health;
- Google Search Console coverage and search performance, if connected;
- referral traffic from outreach placements;
- broken links, sitemap health, QA, and deployment health.

Decision rule for title/description changes:

- do not rewrite pages based on one or two impressions;
- consider testing when a page reaches roughly 50–100 impressions for a coherent query set;
- record the original title/description and the change date;
- allow enough time to evaluate the result before changing it again.

## 7. Outreach evaluation checklist

Before contacting or accepting a placement, check:

- Is the site topically relevant?
- Is the linking page useful and indexable?
- Does the site exercise editorial control?
- Would the link help a human reader even if search engines did not exist?
- Is the requested anchor text natural?
- Is there a demand for payment solely for a ranking link?
- Does the site resemble a link farm, expired-domain network, or mass guest-post marketplace?
- Would Carrowmont be comfortable explaining the placement publicly?

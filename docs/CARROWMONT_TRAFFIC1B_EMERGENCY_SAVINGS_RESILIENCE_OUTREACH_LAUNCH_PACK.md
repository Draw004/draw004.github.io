# CARROWMONT TRAFFIC1B - EMERGENCY SAVINGS & INCOME-LOSS RESILIENCE OUTREACH LAUNCH PACK

## One-sentence finding

Federal Reserve SHED data show that 55% of U.S. adults reported three months of rainy-day savings in 2025, while Carrowmont's transparent runway matrix shows why the dollar amount behind “three months” varies sharply with household cash burn.

## Useful factual angles

- Only 55% of adults reported three months of emergency savings; 30% said they could not cover three months by any available means.
- What $10,000 of accessible savings covers when the monthly cash shortfall is $1,500, $2,500, $3,500 or $5,000.
- Why “three months” is a time target rather than one universal dollar amount.
- The Federal Reserve relationship between having money left over each month and having a three-month rainy-day fund.

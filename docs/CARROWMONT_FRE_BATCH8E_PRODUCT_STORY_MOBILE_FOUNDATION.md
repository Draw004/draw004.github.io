# Carrowmont FRE Batch 8E — Product Story & Mobile-First Foundation

## Purpose
Batch 8E changes how Carrowmont presents the product without changing the financial engines. The public site explains what Carrowmont helps people do before asking them to browse a long list of calculators. My Carrowmont is easier to use from a phone and ready to be installed as a web app.

## Final product story
The homepage presents one connected journey: **Plan / Keep / Track / Understand**. The stages are part of one visual path rather than four numbered feature cards.

- **Plan:** use Carrowmont tools to work through a real financial decision.
- **Keep:** save only the plans that matter in My Carrowmont.
- **Track:** see whether saved plans are improving, on track or ready for review.
- **Understand:** see why something needs attention and return to the right planner to test a change.

The existing calculators remain fully visible and usable. Batch 8E does not perform the later Batch 11 Ask-first homepage transformation.

## One My Carrowmont story, not repeated promotion
The illustrative **See Carrowmont in action** section is the homepage's single primary My Carrowmont showcase. The earlier duplicate My Carrowmont promotional card is removed. Carrowmont Money in Real Life remains an independent newsletter section after the product preview and before decision-support tools.

## Homepage clarity rule
An important product idea should be said once in the strongest place. The duplicate four-benefit trust ribbon is removed. The lower Why Carrowmont section is reduced to three distinct principles:

- Transparent by design.
- Explore before deciding.
- Built for different financial lives.

## Shared header lock
The approved Country/Currency control is a shared component: globe SVG, localized country, middle dot, localized currency and SVG chevron inside the established rounded pill. My Carrowmont uses the same canonical markup as the homepage; legacy text glyphs are not allowed.

## Mobile-first foundation
My Carrowmont includes compact phone quick access for Progress, Saved plans, Financial picture and Saved details. The connected product journey becomes a vertical path on narrow screens rather than a stack of unrelated cards.

## Installable web app
Carrowmont includes a standards-based web app manifest with 192 px and 512 px brand icons, standalone display mode and shortcuts to My Carrowmont, Ask Carrowmont and Tools. No service worker or offline financial-data cache is introduced. Installation improves access only; it does not change what account data is saved.

## Protected architecture
- No calculator formula changes.
- No new financial data collection.
- No bank connection.
- No offline financial-data caching.
- No new public content URL requiring search indexing.
- Existing guest-first tools and optional My Carrowmont account model remain.
- The major Ask-first homepage transformation remains reserved for Batch 11.

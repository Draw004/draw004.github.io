# TRAFFIC1A Mortgage Affordability Monitor - Outreach Launch Pack

## One-sentence finding
At Freddie Mac's 22 October 2026 30-year fixed reference rate of 7.32%, a standardized $400,000 mortgage produces about $2,748 per month in principal and interest, roughly $300 more per month than at the comparable 6.19% rate one year earlier.

## Three key numbers
1. **7.32%** - current Freddie Mac 30-year fixed PMMS reference (22 Oct 2026).
2. **$300/month** - standardized $400,000 payment increase versus the comparable year-ago-rate illustration.
3. **29.7%** - standardized P&I-only payment on the latest median-new-home reference as a share of 2025 gross median monthly household income; not a lender DTI.

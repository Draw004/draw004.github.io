Privacy policy

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how Carrowmont approaches personal information, calculator inputs, website usage information and other data associated with use of the platform.

Privacy by default: Carrowmont calculators can be used without creating an account. The financial values you enter are used to calculate your results and are not used for advertising, profiling or website analytics.

Your financial information

The numbers you enter into Carrowmont calculators are used to calculate your results. Carrowmont does not use those financial values for advertising, profiling or website analytics.

Calculator data and local processing

Carrowmont’s current calculators run in your browser. Some tools may store preferences or saved-plan information locally on your device so the site can provide a more consistent experience. Unless a feature clearly tells you that information is being saved or synced to an account, calculator values are not uploaded to a Carrowmont account.

My Carrowmont saved planning profile

My Carrowmont is an optional account-backed planning profile. You can use Carrowmont calculators, Ask Carrowmont, Learn and Research without creating an account. Guest calculator use does not by itself create or update a My Carrowmont profile.

Only planning information you explicitly choose to keep, or changes you make while signed in to My Carrowmont, become part of your saved profile. A temporary browser session copy may be used while you are signed in so the dashboard can operate, and that account-backed dashboard copy is cleared from the My Carrowmont browser view when you sign out. My Carrowmont does not connect to bank accounts or monitor transactions.

Country and currency preferences

The website may remember your selected country and currency in browser storage so that supported tools can provide a consistent experience.

Website analytics

Carrowmont uses privacy-focused, aggregate Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand site usage and performance. These analytics do not include the financial values you enter into calculators.

Site search

Search words are matched in your browser against Carrowmont's own content index. They are not put in the URL, not saved as search history, and not sent to an external search provider.

What you choose to save

Only plans or updates you explicitly keep become part of My Carrowmont. Unsaved calculations do not become saved plan history.

Sign-in and email delivery

My Carrowmont uses passwordless email sign-in through Supabase. Brevo delivers authentication emails.

More technical details about My Carrowmont

My Carrowmont uses Supabase for passwordless email authentication and for the planning profile or saved scenarios you choose to keep. Carrowmont does not receive or store a password for this passwordless sign-in flow.

If you explicitly select “Save to My Carrowmont” from a supported planning experience, the selected planning information is staged in your browser only long enough to complete the sign-in handoff and is then added to your My Carrowmont profile. Changes you make inside My Carrowmont while signed in are saved to that account-backed profile. When you explicitly update an existing saved plan, My Carrowmont may keep a limited recent history of the versions you chose to save so it can show what changed over time. It may also keep simple plan information such as when the plan started, when you last reviewed it and a target date or target amount when that information is part of the plan. Calculations you do not choose to save are not added to that plan history or target record. Saved account rows are protected by Row Level Security so authenticated users can access only rows owned by their account.

Authentication emails are sent from Carrowmont <no-reply@carrowmont.com> through Brevo SMTP. Supabase processes authentication and saved profile data on Carrowmont’s behalf, and Brevo processes the authentication email delivery on Carrowmont’s behalf. Saved planning information is used to provide the My Carrowmont experience; Carrowmont does not sell those financial planning values or use them for advertising. You can sign out, export saved account data, or delete the saved profile, saved scenarios and their saved-version history. Deleting the saved profile does not automatically delete the underlying authentication identity.

Supabase’s privacy information is available at supabase.com/privacy, and Brevo’s privacy information is available at brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy.

Communications

If you contact Carrowmont by email, the information you send may be used to respond to your enquiry and maintain appropriate correspondence records.

Newsletter subscriptions

Carrowmont Money in Real Life is an optional email newsletter. If you choose to subscribe, Carrowmont uses Kit to collect and store your email address, send the confirmation email and deliver newsletter messages. The newsletter uses a confirmation step before a new address is added to the active mailing list. You can unsubscribe using the link included in newsletter emails.

Kit processes subscriber information on Carrowmont's behalf to provide the newsletter service. The embedded signup form may also process technical information needed to operate and protect the form, such as device, browser, network or usage information. You can read more in Kit's Data Processing Addendum and Kit's Privacy Policy.

Newsletter signup is separate from Carrowmont calculator use and from the optional My Carrowmont account. Carrowmont does not send calculator inputs or calculated financial results to Kit as part of newsletter signup.

Future services

If Carrowmont later introduces payment services, connected financial accounts or other features that collect additional information, this policy will be updated to explain those practices before or when those features are introduced.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be processed when you visit or use Carrowmont and how we intend to approach privacy as the platform develops.

Carrowmont is currently an early-stage financial education and planning website. Some features described in this policy may be introduced as the platform develops.

Information you provide

At present, most Carrowmont tools are designed to operate without requiring users to create an account.

If you voluntarily provide information to Carrowmont in the future, for example through a contact form, newsletter subscription, account registration or other interactive feature, we may process the information necessary to provide that service.

Depending on the feature, this information could include:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • messages or enquiries you send
  • account information
  • preferences you choose to save

Information entered into calculators

Carrowmont financial calculators may ask users to enter financial information in order to generate calculations or projections.

Examples may include income, expenses, savings, investment amounts, retirement age or other financial assumptions.

Where a calculator operates entirely within your browser and does not transmit or store those inputs on a Carrowmont server, the information remains part of the local calculation.

If Carrowmont later introduces accounts, saved plans or cloud-based calculations, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how that information is stored and used.

Technical information

When you visit a website, certain technical information may be processed automatically by hosting providers, internet infrastructure providers or security services.

This can include information such as:

  • IP address
  • browser type
  • device type
  • operating system
  • date and time of requests
  • pages requested
  • referring website or page

Carrowmont is currently hosted using third-party internet infrastructure, and those providers may process standard technical information as part of operating and securing their services.

Cookies

Carrowmont may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, security, analytics or user preferences.

At the current stage of the website, Carrowmont does not rely on advertising cookies or personalized advertising technologies.

If analytics, advertising or other cookie-based technologies are introduced, this policy and any appropriate cookie notices will be updated.

Analytics

Carrowmont may introduce website analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site and which tools or pages are most useful.

Analytics may collect information such as page views, approximate location, device type, referral source and interactions with the website.

When analytics services are implemented, this Privacy Policy will be updated where appropriate to identify relevant providers and explain how the information is used.

Advertising and affiliate relationships

Carrowmont may in the future generate revenue through advertising, sponsorships, affiliate relationships, referrals or partnerships.

Some of these services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure referrals or advertising activity.

Where such services are introduced, appropriate privacy and commercial disclosures will be added.

Third-party links

Carrowmont may link to websites or services operated by third parties.

Those websites have their own privacy practices and policies. Carrowmont is not responsible for how third-party websites collect or process information.

Users should review the privacy policy of any external service they choose to use.

How information may be used

Information processed by Carrowmont may be used where appropriate to:

  • operate and maintain the website
  • provide requested features or services
  • respond to enquiries
  • improve tools and educational content
  • understand website usage
  • protect the website from abuse or security threats
  • comply with applicable legal requirements

Sale of personal information

Carrowmont does not currently operate a business model based on selling users' personal information.

Data security

Reasonable efforts should be made to protect information processed by Carrowmont.

However, no internet service or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Carrowmont is intended primarily for adults interested in financial planning and education.

The website is not currently designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your privacy choices

As Carrowmont introduces features that involve personal information, users may have rights or choices relating to their information depending on applicable law and their location.

Relevant procedures for exercising those rights will be added as necessary when those features are introduced.

Changes to this policy

Carrowmont will continue to evolve, and our privacy practices may change as new tools and services are introduced.

This Privacy Policy may therefore be updated periodically.

The latest version will be published on this page together with an updated revision date.

Contact

A dedicated Carrowmont contact channel for privacy enquiries will be added as the platform's communication infrastructure is established.

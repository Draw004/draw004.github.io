Privacy policy

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how Carrowmont approaches personal information, calculator inputs, website usage information and other data associated with use of the platform.

Carrowmont is currently an early-stage financial education and planning website. The platform uses privacy-first aggregate traffic measurement and also offers optional Google Analytics for deeper usage insights, as described below. This Privacy Policy will be updated as the platform evolves.

Calculator information

Carrowmont’s current browser-based tools may store selected preferences or saved-plan information locally on your device. Unless a feature explicitly states otherwise, locally stored calculator data is not uploaded to a Carrowmont account.

Country and currency preferences

The website may remember your selected country and currency in browser storage so that supported tools can provide a consistent experience.

Privacy-first aggregate traffic analytics

Carrowmont uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand broad website traffic trends such as page views, visits, referrers and general technical or geographic patterns. Cloudflare Web Analytics is designed to provide aggregate website statistics without using cookies, localStorage or fingerprinting to identify individual visitors. Carrowmont does not intentionally send calculator inputs or financial values through this traffic measurement.

Optional Google Analytics

Carrowmont also uses Google Analytics 4 only if you choose Accept Analytics. Google Analytics helps us understand deeper website engagement, such as page visits, broad device and traffic information, scrolling and similar usage so we can improve the site and its tools. Carrowmont does not intentionally send calculator inputs or financial values to Google Analytics. If you decline analytics, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded. Your choice is stored in your browser and can be changed at any time using the button below.

Communications

If you contact Carrowmont by email, the information you send may be used to respond to your enquiry and maintain appropriate correspondence records.

Future services

If Carrowmont later introduces accounts, newsletters, payment services or other features that collect additional information, this policy will be updated to explain those practices.

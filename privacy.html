Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how Carrowmont approaches personal information, calculator inputs, website usage information and other data associated with use of the platform.

Carrowmont is currently an early-stage financial education and planning website. The platform will continue to evolve, and this Privacy Policy will be updated when new features involving personal information, analytics, accounts or other services are introduced.

Information you provide

At present, most Carrowmont tools are intended to operate without requiring users to create an account.

If you voluntarily communicate with Carrowmont, we may receive information that you choose to provide.

This may include:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • the contents of messages you send
  • feedback or questions
  • information you voluntarily provide in connection with an enquiry

Information entered into calculators

Carrowmont financial tools may ask users to enter information needed to perform calculations or financial scenario analysis.

Depending on the tool, this might include information such as:

  • income
  • expenses
  • savings or investments
  • financial goals
  • ages and time horizons
  • retirement assumptions
  • inflation assumptions
  • expected financial returns

Where a calculator operates entirely inside your browser and does not transmit or store those inputs on a Carrowmont server, the information remains part of the local calculation.

If Carrowmont later introduces saved plans, user accounts, server-based calculations or cloud storage, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how relevant information is collected, stored and used.

Sensitive information

Users should avoid sending passwords, bank account credentials, card details, authentication codes or other highly sensitive information through ordinary email or website communications.

Technical information

When a person visits a website, certain technical information may be processed automatically by hosting providers, internet infrastructure providers, security services or the user's browser.

Depending on the service involved, this information can include:

  • IP address
  • browser type
  • device type
  • operating system
  • date and time of requests
  • pages requested
  • referring website or page

Carrowmont currently uses third-party internet infrastructure to provide the website and domain services.

Cookies

Carrowmont may use cookies or similar technologies where required for website functionality, security, analytics or user preferences.

At the current stage of the platform, Carrowmont does not intentionally use personalized advertising cookies on the website.

If analytics, advertising or other technologies using cookies are introduced, this policy and any relevant notices will be updated where appropriate.

Analytics

Carrowmont may introduce analytics services to understand how visitors use the website and which tools or educational resources are most useful.

Analytics services may process information such as:

  • pages viewed
  • approximate geographic region
  • device and browser information
  • referral source
  • interactions with website features

When analytics services are implemented, this Privacy Policy will be updated where appropriate to identify relevant services and explain their use.

Email communications

Carrowmont operates the business email address contact@carrowmont.com for general enquiries, feedback and communication.

If you email Carrowmont, your email address, message and related technical information may be processed by the email provider used to operate the mailbox.

Email correspondence may be retained where reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain business records, protect Carrowmont or comply with applicable requirements.

Advertising and affiliate relationships

Carrowmont may in the future generate revenue through advertising, sponsorships, affiliate relationships, referrals, partnerships or subscriptions.

Some of these services may use cookies or other technologies to measure advertising performance, referrals or website activity.

Appropriate privacy and commercial disclosures will be introduced when such services are implemented.

Third-party websites

Carrowmont may contain links to websites and services operated by third parties.

Those services operate independently and have their own privacy practices and policies.

Users should review the privacy practices of third-party services before providing them with personal information.

How information may be used

Information processed by Carrowmont may be used where appropriate to:

  • operate and maintain the website
  • provide requested features or services
  • respond to questions and feedback
  • investigate technical problems
  • improve financial tools and educational content
  • understand website usage
  • protect the platform from fraud, abuse or security threats
  • comply with applicable legal requirements

Sale of personal information

Carrowmont does not currently operate a business model based on selling users' personal information.

Data security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information processed in connection with Carrowmont.

However, no internet service, email system or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, protecting the platform or complying with applicable requirements.

Children's privacy

Carrowmont is intended primarily for adults interested in financial planning and education.

The platform is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your privacy choices

Depending on applicable law and a user's location, individuals may have rights regarding personal information associated with them.

As Carrowmont introduces features involving greater collection or storage of personal information, procedures for relevant privacy requests will be developed where required.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

Carrowmont will continue to evolve and its privacy practices may change as new tools and services are introduced.

This Privacy Policy may therefore be updated periodically.

The latest version will be published on this page together with its revision date.

Privacy questions

Questions concerning this Privacy Policy or Carrowmont's handling of information may be sent to:

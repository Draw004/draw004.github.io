Privacy policy

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how Carrowmont approaches personal information, calculator inputs, website usage information and other data associated with use of the platform.

Privacy by default: Carrowmont calculators can be used without creating an account and without enabling Google Analytics. Financial values you enter into calculators are kept separate from website analytics. Optional analytics and any future account or cloud-saving features are separate choices.

Calculator data and local processing

Carrowmont’s current calculators run in your browser. Some tools may store preferences or saved-plan information locally on your device so the site can provide a more consistent experience. Unless a feature explicitly tells you that information is being saved or synced to an account, calculator values are not uploaded to a Carrowmont account.

Country and currency preferences

The website may remember your selected country and currency in browser storage so that supported tools can provide a consistent experience.

Analytics and your financial data

Carrowmont keeps calculator financial values separate from analytics. The analytics implementation is restricted to general website-usage information and approved interaction events. Optional Google Analytics page-location reporting is limited to the page path rather than query-string values, and Carrowmont’s analytics events do not include calculator amounts, balances, income, spending, contributions, ages, interest rates, return assumptions or calculated results.

Privacy-first aggregate traffic measurement

Carrowmont uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand broad website traffic trends such as page views, visits, referrers and general technical or geographic patterns. Carrowmont’s calculator code does not pass calculator financial input values to this aggregate traffic measurement.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is loaded only if you choose Allow Google Analytics. When enabled, Carrowmont may use it for general interactions such as opening a tool, generating a report, copying a summary, changing country or currency, or switching calculator modes. Financial values and calculated results are not included in those analytics events. If you choose Continue without Google Analytics, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded. Your choice is stored in your browser and can be changed at any time using the button below.

Accounts and saved financial information

Carrowmont’s core calculators currently do not require an account. If optional accounts, cloud saving or synced plans are introduced, the feature will explain what information is stored and why before you choose to use it. Account or saved-plan information will be handled separately from website analytics. Allowing Google Analytics will not, by itself, give permission to upload calculator inputs or use saved financial information as analytics data.

Communications

If you contact Carrowmont by email, the information you send may be used to respond to your enquiry and maintain appropriate correspondence records.

Future services

If Carrowmont later introduces newsletters, payment services or other features that collect additional information, this policy will be updated to explain those practices before or when those features are introduced.
